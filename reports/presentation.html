1. The Business Problem

The Core Challenge: Over 84% of e-commerce sessions abandon carts without buying.

The Costly Mistake: Blanketing all visitors with coupons wastes margins on organic buyers and burns marketing budget on casual visitors.

The Solution: Real-time session classification (<5ms) to predict intent and trigger dynamic actions (Protect Margin vs 15% Exit Voucher vs Live Chat).

2. Data Understanding

  • Source: UCI Machine Learning Repository (12,330 real browsing sessions).
  • Features: 18 columns (Page views, durations, bounce rates, exit rates, GA PageValues, visitor type).
  • Severe Imbalance: 84.5% non-buyers vs 15.5% buyers (5.46:1 ratio).
  • Key Lesson: Standard accuracy is misleading; a dummy baseline gets 84.5% accuracy with zero business utility.

3. Key EDA Insights

  • Google Analytics PageValues: 78.4% of purchasers visit pages with positive PageValues (avg score: 27.2 vs 1.9 for abandoners).
  • Bounce & Exit Rates: Abandoners experience 3x higher bounce rates and 2.5x higher exit rates.
  • Seasonal Spike: November traffic converts at 25.3% due to holiday shopping.
  • Returning Visitors: Represent >85% of volume; new visitors have higher conversion density when viewing products.

4. Preprocessing & Leakage Prevention

Strict 80/20 Stratified Split: Executed before any scaling, encoding, or fitting to guarantee ZERO data leakage.

Engineered Features:
  • Product_Page_Ratio: Product page views / Total page views
  • Avg_Duration_Per_Page: Total duration / Total pages
  • PageValue_Bounce_Ratio: PageValues / (BounceRates + 1e-4)

10. Summary & Key Takeaways

  • Accuracy is misleading: PR-AUC and Recall are the true metrics for conversion.
  • Domain features win: Behavioral engagement ratios outranked raw duration metrics.
  • Machine Learning delivers real revenue: Connecting predictions to a concrete decision matrix directly protects profits.
